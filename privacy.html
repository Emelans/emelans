Bistroodle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what data Bistroodle processes, why, and what rights you have over it, wherever you play. Section 10 lists the rights that apply in particular regions. Players in Türkiye are also covered by the KVKK Information Notice (in Turkish: KVKK Aydınlatma Metni).

1. Who we are

Bistroodle is made by Emelans, an independent game studio run by Mehmet Emir Yücel in Türkiye, who is the controller of the data described here.

  • Email: emiryucel14@icloud.com

2. In short

  • You don't need a name, email address, phone number or password to play.
  • There are no ads and no purchases in the game. We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • The online part of the game (visits, likes, lists, Ribbons and the Gala Catalog) is optional. It is only switched on if you are 16 or older and choose Accept on the privacy card at the start of the game. An anonymous player account is then created for you, and the restaurant you publish and your in-game interactions are stored on the server under it.
  • If you choose Decline, or you are under 16, you can still play the whole game on your device; nothing is sent to the server. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • You can delete your server data at any time from inside the game: Settings → Account and data → Delete my data. Deleting it also switches the online features off.

3. What data we process

3.2 Kept only on your device

Your save game (your restaurant, money, progress), your sound, language and accessibility preferences, your answer on the privacy card (and, if you are under 16, your birth year, so the question isn't asked again) and the local notifications the game schedules stay on your device and are not sent to us.

3.3 What we don't collect

The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier (IDFA), payment details or any sensitive data.

4. What other players see

When your restaurant is published, other players can see its name, layout and furniture, level, rating, restaurant code, and your score and rank on the like lists. Nothing that identifies you as a person is shared.

You choose your restaurant's name. Please don't put personal information such as your real name, address or phone number in it. Names with offensive words are refused, and names reported by several players are shown to others as a plain "Restaurant".

6. Who we share data with

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services): the server side of the game runs on Unity's player accounts, server code, data storage and leaderboards. Unity processes this data on our behalf and on our instructions, under its data processing terms. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple: the game is distributed through the App Store and TestFlight. Data Apple processes there is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Other players: only what is listed in section 4.
  • Authorities: only where the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information, we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use it to profile you.

7. International transfers

We are based in Türkiye and our provider Unity stores data on servers outside Türkiye, which may also be outside your country (for example in the United States). Transfers are protected as the law of your region requires: for the EEA, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in Unity's data processing terms; for the UK, the UK addendum to those clauses; for Türkiye, the safeguards in Article 9 of the KVKK.

8. How long we keep data

Server data is kept while your account is in use. When you choose "Delete my data", your restaurant, in-game interactions, Ribbon balance and account are deleted from the server straight away, the online features switch off, and other players can no longer open your restaurant. Our provider's technical logs and backups may be kept for a short time longer and are then deleted automatically.

9. Children

The online features are only for players aged 16 and over. The privacy card asks for your birth year before the online features can be switched on; if you are under 16, they stay off, no account is created and nothing is sent to the server. You can still play the whole game on your device.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 (under 13 in the United States, as defined by COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the online features, write to us and we will delete the data. The child can also use "Delete my data" in Settings.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to stop processing it, and you can withdraw your consent. The quickest way to delete everything is Delete my data in the game. For anything else, write to emiryucel14@icloud.com. We answer free of charge within 30 days. Because accounts are anonymous, we may ask for your restaurant code so we can tell the request is yours. We will never treat you differently for using your rights.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom: you also have the rights to restrict processing and to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

United States: this includes the privacy rights given by state laws such as those of California, Virginia and Colorado where they apply to us: to know, access, correct and delete your personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or process sensitive personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We treat browser-level opt-out signals (such as Global Privacy Control) as a request not to sell or share, which we never do anyway.

Brazil: you have the rights in Article 18 of the LGPD, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion, portability and information about sharing, and you can complain to the ANPD.

Türkiye: your rights are set out in the KVKK Information Notice; you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority.

Other countries (for example Canada, Japan, South Korea and Australia): you can use the rights your local law gives you by writing to us, and complain to your local privacy authority.

11. Security

The connection to the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Your server data is kept in a private area that players cannot read or change directly, and the game's rules are enforced on the server.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page and announce important changes in the game. If a change needs your agreement, the game will ask you again.

13. Contact
